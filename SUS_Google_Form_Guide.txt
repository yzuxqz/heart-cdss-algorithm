Google Forms SUS 问卷设置指南
============================

你的系统地址: https://heart-cdss-algorithm.streamlit.app/

打开 https://docs.google.com/forms/ → Blank form → 按下面步骤操作。


一、Form Settings（右上角齿轮图标）
----------------------------------
- Collect email addresses: Disabled
- Limit to 1 response: Disabled（如果你想每人只能填一次就打开）
- Show progress bar: Enabled
- Confirmation message: "Thank you for participating! Your feedback helps improve the system."


二、Form Header
----------------------------------
Title:   Heart CDSS — System Usability Evaluation

Description (直接复制):
---
This questionnaire evaluates the usability of the Heart CDSS prototype, a machine learning-based decision support system for cardiovascular disease risk assessment.

Instructions:
1. Open the system at https://heart-cdss-algorithm.streamlit.app/
2. Complete the following three tasks:
   a. Input a patient's data and view the risk prediction result
   b. Generate a SHAP explanation to understand what drives the prediction
   c. Upload a batch CSV to test the batch scoring feature
3. Return to this form and answer all 10 questions below based on your experience.

All responses are anonymous. There are no right or wrong answers — please answer honestly based on your actual experience with the system.
---


三、Questions
----------------------------------
所有题目类型: Linear scale
Range: 1 to 5
标签: 1 = Strongly Disagree, 5 = Strongly Agree
全部设为 Required (右下角开关打开)

#1: I think that I would like to use this system frequently.

#2: I found the system unnecessarily complex.

#3: I thought the system was easy to use.

#4: I think that I would need the support of a technical person to be able to use this system.

#5: I found the various functions in this system were well integrated.

#6: I thought there was too much inconsistency in this system.

#7: I would imagine that most people would learn to use this system very quickly.

#8: I found the system very cumbersome to use.

#9: I felt very confident using the system.

#10: I needed to learn a lot of things before I could get going with this system.


四、SUS 计分公式（论文分析用）
----------------------------------
Google Forms → Responses → Link to Sheets → 在 Google Sheets 里：

假设 B-K 列依次为 Q1-Q10 的原始评分（A列是时间戳），在 L2 填入：
=((B2-1)+(5-C2)+(D2-1)+(5-E2)+(F2-1)+(5-G2)+(H2-1)+(5-I2)+(J2-1)+(5-K2))*2.5

向下拖动，得到每个受访者的 SUS Score (0-100)。

然后计算: =AVERAGE(L2:L100) → 整体 SUS 得分。
68+ = 可用性良好，80+ = 优秀。

论文中报告格式示例:
"The mean SUS score was 78.5 (SD = 11.2), indicating good usability above the industry benchmark of 68."
